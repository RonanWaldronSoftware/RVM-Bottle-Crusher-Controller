BB01 v34: dual IMX708 direct CSI setup with DotStar LED strip control.

Camera setup:
1. Power off Jetson.
2. Connect IMX708 to CAM0 and IMX708 to CAM1. No UC-475. No IMX219.
3. Run: sudo ./install_dual_imx708_dtb.sh
4. Reboot.
5. Run: ./verify_dual_imx708_dtb.sh and ./check_dual_imx708_direct.sh
6. Run: ./run_dashboard.sh --no-camera-autostart

LED setup:
- Jetson pin 19 MOSI -> DotStar DI / DATA IN
- Jetson pin 23 SCLK -> DotStar CI / CLOCK IN
- Jetson GND -> LED PSU negative / LED GND
- External 5 V PSU positive -> LED strip 5V
- External 5 V PSU negative -> LED strip GND
- Recommended: use a 74AHCT125/74AHCT245 level shifter for DATA and CLOCK.

Dashboard:
Controls tab -> DotStar LED strip lets you set total LEDs, LEDs to light, brightness, colour, and LED OFF.
